Visitor log SAMPLE

Privacy

What the home keeps

Your name and phone number, who you visited, and when you signed in and out.

Why

So staff know who is in the building, for example in a fire drill, and can reach visitors if they need to, for example during an outbreak.

How long

30 days, then it is deleted automatically.

Who sees it

Staff at the home. This app does not send it anywhere.

On this phone

Your name and phone number are saved on this phone so you don't have to type them again. "Not you?" removes them.

No health information about residents is stored, and your screening answers are not saved.

Questions: ask at the nurse's desk.
